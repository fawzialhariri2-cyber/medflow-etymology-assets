# MedFlow Workshop — image integration rules

## Global
- Use **CONTAIN**, not destructive COVER, unless a slide is intentionally a background hero and the teaching target remains fully visible.
- Never crop away the defining analogy shape or anatomical target.
- If the frame ratio does not match, use an internal matte/letterbox (deep navy or ivory) rather than cropping.
- Never stretch or distort anatomy.
- Never duplicate the same image on one slide simply to fill space.
- Anatomy must be large enough to identify instantly.
- Analogy object should have a clean, unmistakable silhouette.
- If a URL fails, keep the current design and report the filename; do not insert a placeholder.

## Families
1. Arcus — full bow/arch + anatomical arcuate curve.
2. Sagitta — full arrow + superior skull with clear sagittal suture.
3. Falx — full sickle + clearly visible falx cerebri between hemispheres.
4. Trochlea — full pulley/groove + clear anatomical trochlea.
5. Serra — prominent saw teeth + serratus anterior slips.
6. Malleus/Malleolus — full hammer + clearly identifiable malleus; do not split Malleolus into a separate lesson.
7. Incus — full anvil + isolated/highlighted incus.
8. Stapes/Stapedius — full stirrup + clearly visible stapes.
9. Stylos/Styloid — full stylus/pointed pillar + clearly visible styloid process.
10. Vomer — plowshare + vomer with enough septal/skull context.
11. Xiphos/Xiphoid — full short sword + sternum with xiphoid highlighted.
12. Clavis/Clavicula/Clavicle — full ancient key + complete clavicle, both ends visible, S-curve preserved.
